Manage people

Household people organize financial accounts. Adding a person does not grant login access, require an email address, or require Plaid keys.

Account holders, beneficiaries and responsible adults are separate relationships chosen on each account. Login access stays in your Cloudflare Access policy.

D1 is the source of truth after the initial import from MINTY_USERS. Names and order can change here; internal IDs and existing Plaid credential names never change. Archived people keep their account references.
